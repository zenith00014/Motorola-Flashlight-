How to use

1Tap Activate. Allow camera access when Chrome asks. The camera is used only to control its LED flash.
2Make two quick, firm chopping or shaking movements with your phone.
3The flashlight turns on. Tap Stop to turn it off and release the camera.

Keep this page open and the screen on. A webpage cannot reliably run this gesture service while locked or in the background.

Requires Android browser support for motion sensors and camera torch control.
